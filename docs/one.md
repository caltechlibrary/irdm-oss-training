### Agenda

* 9:00– 10:00 AM — FAIR, Repositories, & Discovery
  * Types and role of repositories as open-scholarship enablers to make research Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable (FAIR).
  * Repository core functions: metadata, curation, long-term preservation, access, and discovery.
  * Institutional repositories are more sustainable and long-lasting than other repositories.
* 10:00 – 11:00 AM — Metadata Schemas & Interoperability
  * Types of metadata: descriptive, technical, administrative.
  * Overview of metadata standards: DataCite (v4.5–v4.7 updates), Dublin Core, MARC XML, JSON-LD. Also go over OAI-PMH harvest.
  * Structuring dataset descriptions and resource types (publications, datasets, software, study registrations) to match different needs.
* 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM — Persistent Identifiers (PIDs)
  * Registering DOIs via DataCite and Crossref.
  * Author and institutional PIDs: ORCID integration, ROR (Research Organization Registry) affiliations, and validating identifier syntax with Python libraries like `idutils`.
